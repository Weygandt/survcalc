Triangle Solutions
SSS — Three sides known. Law of cosines: cos A = (b²+c²−a²)/(2bc)
SAS — Two sides and included angle. Law of cosines: c² = a²+b²−2ab·cos C
ASA — Two angles and included side. Law of sines: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C
AAS — Two angles and non-included side. C = 180°−A−B, then law of sines.
Right triangle — enter any two knowns. C = 90°, sides a (opposite A), b (adjacent to A), hypotenuse c.
